### What should I wear?
Traditional Indian attire is warmly welcomed: silk sarees, lehengas, kurtas or dhotis. Bright colours are great! Guests often avoid all-black or all-white.

### Do I need to take off my shoes?
Yes, usually for the ceremony area (the _mantapa_). Easy-to-remove footwear is a good idea.

### How long is the ceremony?
The muhurtha rituals run for a few hours in the morning. Feel free to come and go quietly, but please try to be there for the Dhare Muhurtha.

### What food will be served?
A traditional vegetarian Karnataka meal, served on banana leaves.
