Ours is a Kannada Smartha Brahmin wedding. Here is a short guide to what you'll see, especially if this is your first South Indian wedding.

### Naandi · <span lang="kn">ನಾಂದಿ</span>
The opening ritual. Both families pray to the family deities and ancestors for blessings so the wedding goes smoothly.

### Vara Pooje · <span lang="kn">ವರ ಪೂಜೆ</span>
The bride's family formally welcomes the groom and his family and honours him.

### Kashi Yatre · <span lang="kn">ಕಾಶಿ ಯಾತ್ರೆ</span>
A playful favourite. The groom, with umbrella, walking stick and fan, pretends to leave for Kashi to lead a life of study and renunciation. The bride's brother (or father) stops him and persuades him to marry instead.

### Gowri Pooje · <span lang="kn">ಗೌರಿ ಪೂಜೆ</span>
The bride prays to Goddess Gowri (Parvati) for a happy married life while the groom's rituals take place.

### Dhare Muhurtha & Kanyadaana · <span lang="kn">ಧಾರೆ ಮುಹೂರ್ತ ಮತ್ತು ಕನ್ಯಾದಾನ</span>
The heart of the ceremony, performed at the auspicious moment. The bride's parents pour sacred water (_dhare_) over the couple's joined hands, giving their daughter's hand in marriage.

### Jeerige Bella · <span lang="kn">ಜೀರಿಗೆ ಬೆಲ್ಲ</span>
At the exact muhurtha, the bride and groom place a paste of cumin and jaggery on each other's heads, a wish for a life that is both a little bitter and very sweet, lived together.

### Mangalya Dharane · <span lang="kn">ಮಾಂಗಲ್ಯ ಧಾರಣೆ</span>
The groom ties the _mangalasutra_ around the bride's neck. Guests shower the couple with _akshate_ (<span lang="kn">ಅಕ್ಷತೆ</span>), turmeric-coloured rice, as a blessing. You'll be handed some!

### Laaja Homa · <span lang="kn">ಲಾಜ ಹೋಮ</span>
The bride's brother gives her puffed rice, which the couple offers into the sacred fire.

### Saptapadi · <span lang="kn">ಸಪ್ತಪದಿ</span>
The couple take seven steps together around the fire. Each step is a vow: for nourishment, strength, prosperity, happiness, children, long life, and lifelong friendship.

### Arundhati Darshana · <span lang="kn">ಅರುಂಧತಿ ದರ್ಶನ</span>
The groom shows the bride the star Arundhati, a symbol of steadfast devotion.

### Okuli & games · <span lang="kn">ಓಕುಳಿ</span>
Light-hearted games such as finding a ring in coloured water, which the families love to watch.
