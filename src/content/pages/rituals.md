timeline:
  - label: Day 1 · Morning
    title: Naandi
    titleKn: ನಾಂದಿ
    text: The opening ritual. Both families pray to the family deities and ancestors for their blessings so the wedding goes smoothly.

  - label: Day 1 · Evening
    title: Vara Pooje
    titleKn: ವರ ಪೂಜೆ
    text: The bride's family formally welcomes the groom and his family and honours him, often followed by the <em>Nischitartha</em> (formal engagement).

  - label: Day 2 · Dawn
    title: Kashi Yatre
    titleKn: ಕಾಶಿ ಯಾತ್ರೆ
    text: A playful favourite. The groom, carrying an umbrella, walking stick and fan, pretends to leave for Kashi to lead a life of study and renunciation, until the bride's brother persuades him to marry instead.

  - label: Day 2 · Morning
    title: Gowri Pooje
    titleKn: ಗೌರಿ ಪೂಜೆ
    text: The bride prays to Goddess Gowri (Parvati) for a happy married life while the groom's rituals take place.

  - label: The Muhurtha
    title: Dhare & Kanyadaana
    titleKn: ಧಾರೆ ಮತ್ತು ಕನ್ಯಾದಾನ
    text: The heart of the ceremony, at the auspicious moment. The bride's parents pour sacred water (<em>dhare</em>) over the couple's joined hands, giving their daughter's hand in marriage.

  - label: The Muhurtha
    title: Jeerige Bella
    titleKn: ಜೀರಿಗೆ ಬೆಲ್ಲ
    text: At the exact muhurtha, the couple place a paste of cumin and jaggery on each other's heads, a wish for a life that is a little bitter and very sweet, lived together.

  - label: The Muhurtha
    title: Mangalya Dharane
    titleKn: ಮಾಂಗಲ್ಯ ಧಾರಣೆ
    text: The groom ties the <em>mangalasutra</em> around the bride's neck, and guests shower the couple with <em>akshate</em>, turmeric-coloured rice. You'll be handed some!

  - label: After the Muhurtha
    title: Laaja Homa
    titleKn: ಲಾಜ ಹೋಮ
    text: The bride's brother gives her puffed rice, which the couple offers into the sacred fire.

  - label: After the Muhurtha
    title: Saptapadi
    titleKn: ಸಪ್ತಪದಿ
    text: Seven steps around the fire, each a vow for nourishment, strength, prosperity, happiness, children, long life, and lifelong friendship.

  - label: Evening
    title: Arundhati Darshana
    titleKn: ಅರುಂಧತಿ ದರ್ಶನ
    text: The groom shows the bride the star Arundhati, a symbol of steadfast devotion.

  - label: Just for fun
    title: Okuli & Games
    titleKn: ಓಕುಳಿ
    text: Light-hearted games such as finding a ring in a pot of coloured water, which the families love to cheer on.
---
